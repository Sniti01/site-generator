    title: Who Max Payne is
    meta: The man at the center of all three games, 2001 to 2012
    body:
      - >-
        Max Payne is a New York detective whose life fell apart in 1998. Three
        men out of their minds on Valkyr, a designer drug fresh on the street,
        broke into his home in New Jersey and killed his wife, Michelle, and
        their baby daughter, Rose.
        Max shot all three of them, too late to save his family.
      - >-
        The loss sends him to the Drug Enforcement Administration and deep
        undercover in the mob, and it haunts him long after. In
        the first one, Max tells the story himself, looking back. Sam Lake
        created him — the name was originally Max Heat — and wrote the first
        two games; James McCaffrey voiced him in all three.

    meta: The first game, up to its big reveal

      - >-
        Three years after the murders, Max is working undercover inside the
        Punchinello crime family, which runs the Valkyr trade. A DEA colleague,
        B.B., sets up a meeting in a subway station with Max’s friend Alex
        Balder. Alex is shot by a killer nobody sees, and the murder is pinned

      - >-

        ladder: Vinnie Gognitti, the right-hand man of underboss Jack Lupino,
        then Lupino himself. He runs into Mona Sax, a contract killer whose twin
        sister is married to the don, makes a deal with the Russian mobster
        Vladimir Lem, and reaches Don Angelo Punchinello only to find that the
        don is a puppet for someone bigger.
  - id: valkyr
    year: Valkyr
    title: The drug behind everything
    meta: The first game’s central reveal
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Valkyr, or “V” on the street, is named after the valkyries of Norse
        mythology, and it did not start in a back-room lab. It came out of the
        Valhalla Project, a military program from the Gulf War years that tried
        to raise soldiers’ stamina and morale with chemicals. It was shut down
        after poor results; Nicole Horne quietly brought it back through Aesir,
        her pharmaceutical company.
      - >-
        That is why Max’s family died: Michelle had stumbled onto Aesir’s
        illegal tests, and Horne ordered her killed. B.B. was on Horne’s
        payroll and set Max up. Watching from the shadows is the Inner Circle,
        a secret society led by Senator Alfred Woden. Horne was once a member,
        and Woden offers to take care of the charges against Max if he kills
        her. The whole game leans on Norse myth — Ragnarök above all, and names
        like Valkyr, Valhalla and Aesir.

        war with Woden’s. Max’s new partner, Detective Valerie Winterson, is not
        what she seems. Where the first game drew on Norse myth, Lake wrote the
        sequel as a noir love story, with Max and Mona at its center.

  - id: characters
    year: Who’s who
    title: The people around Max
    meta: Allies, enemies and the ones who were both
    body:
      - >-
        Mona Sax is a contract killer and the other half of the series’ doomed
        romance. The first game introduces her as the twin of Lisa, the abused
        wife of Don Punchinello; in the sequel she is playable in four chapters.
        Julia Murney and Wendy Hoopes voiced her, and Mila Kunis played her in
        the 2008 movie.
      - >-
        Vladimir Lem, a smooth, old-school Russian gangster, is Max’s ally in
        the first game. Vinnie Gognitti starts out as Lupino’s right-hand man
        and returns in the sequel as underboss. Nicole Horne runs Aesir and the
        Valkyr business, and Senator Alfred Woden leads the Inner Circle; Sam
        Lake’s mother played Horne and his father played Woden in the first
        game. In Brazil, Raul Passos is the partner whose loyalty Max can never
        quite count on.
  - id: endings
    year: Spoilers
    title: How each game ends
    meta: Skip this row if you have not finished all three games
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Max Payne: as Horne tries to escape by helicopter, Max cuts the guy
        wires holding up the building’s antenna and brings it down on her. He
        surrenders to the NYPD, and outside, in the crowd, he spots Woden and
        smiles.
      - >-
        Max Payne 2: the Cleaners are Vlad’s tool for wiping out his rivals, and
        Winterson was his lover, secretly on his side. At Woden’s mansion, Vlad
        shoots Mona and kills Woden, and Max kills Vlad as the house burns.
        Mona dies in Max’s arms — unless you finish the game on Dead on Arrival,
        its hardest setting, where she survives. Rockstar’s Dan Houser said Max
        Payne 3 would not carry that thread on.
      - >-
        Max Payne 3: the kidnapping was never the real story. Victor had
        Rodrigo killed for his fortune and was funding a run for mayor with an
        organ-harvesting ring fed with prisoners by the UFE, São Paulo’s police
        tactical unit. Passos, who had taken Victor’s money, comes back to save
        Max. At the airport, Max and Detective Da Silva wreck Victor’s private
        jet. A week later, in Bahia, Max hears that Victor has been found dead
        in prison and walks the beach at sunset, finally at peace.
  - id: alan-wake-2
    year: Alan Wake 2
    title: Is Max Payne in Alan Wake 2?
    meta: Alex Casey, Sam Lake’s face and James McCaffrey’s voice
    body:
      - >-
        The Escapist’s answer is no, with a catch. In Remedy’s Alan Wake
        (2010), Alex Casey, the New York detective of Alan Wake’s famous crime
        novels, was a playful nod to Max Payne. Alan Wake 2 (2023) has an FBI
        agent named Alex Casey, Saga Anderson’s partner, with Sam Lake’s face,
        the one the first Max had, and McCaffrey’s voice. Game Rant’s verdict:
        not Max on paper, the same man in all but name.
      - >-
        Remedy’s connected universe, confirmed with Control in 2019, takes in
        Alan Wake but not Max Payne, whose rights, as of September 2026, belong
        to Rockstar. Lake
        himself, The Escapist reports, has put the Max Payne games outside it.
        An Easter egg in the first Alan Wake says Max died in 2016; Wikipedia
        does not treat it as canon.

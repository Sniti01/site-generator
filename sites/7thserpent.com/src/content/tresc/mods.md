  - id: kung-fu-mod
    year: Max Payne · 2003
    title: The Kung Fu mod
    meta: Kung Fu Edition 3.0 by Kenneth Yeung
    body:
      - >-
        Rock Paper Shotgun counts Kenneth Yeung’s Kung Fu Edition among the
        best fan additions to the first game. It began as a personal project
        in 2001, and version 3.0 came out in 2003. It brings the style of Hong
        Kong kung fu films to Max Payne: fists and a fighting staff, more than
        20 moves with combos and a skill level that grows, wall runs and wall
        jumps, two new shootdodges, bodies you can juggle, and a dojo that
        teaches the basics.
      - >-
        To play it, unzip the mod into the folder with MaxPayne.exe, start the
        game and pick it under Choose Customized Games in the startup window.
  - id: rtx-remix
    year: RTX Remix
    title: Path tracing for the first two games
    meta: Fan remasters made with NVIDIA’s RTX Remix toolkit
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        NVIDIA’s RTX Remix lets modders rebuild old games with full path-traced
        lighting, and both Remedy games have fan projects. In April 2026 the
        modder Neo_minigan released a path-tracing mod for Max Payne —
        DSOGaming counts it as the game’s third — that keeps the original art
        direction and, like the two before it, covers only the prologue and
        Roscoe Street Station. DSOGaming called it an early beta that may show
        visual bugs.
      - >-
        The lighting is expensive. In a January 2024 video of an earlier Remix
        mod, Max Payne fell from over 1,000 frames per second to around 60 on
        an RTX 4080. Darko9.13’s Max Payne 2 RTX Remix, playable in November
        2025 while still in development, adds realistic lighting, physically based materials and
        better textures; at 4K with DLSS 4 on Performance it dropped to about
        40 fps in one scene on an RTX 5080, and 1440p on Balanced came close to
        60.
  - id: max-payne-2-mods
    year: Max Payne 2
    title: Classics for the sequel
    meta: Built with the tools Rockstar and Remedy released
    body:
      - >-
        Rockstar and Remedy put out development tools for Max Payne 2, and
        players used them to add weapons, characters and whole new campaigns.
        Two picks from Rock Paper Shotgun’s 2019 list name their makers. The
        Old School Team’s Max Payne 2: Old School Mod, made for Max’s
        eighteenth anniversary, casts Sam Lake as Max again and redraws almost every comic
        cutscene in the first game’s photo style. Modder Eric Yockey’s
        Marathon//Max brings team matches against bots, modeled on Bungie’s
        Marathon games.
      - >-
        Most Max Payne 2 mods unpack into the game folder and let you choose
        which one to run at launch. Many recommend adding -skipstartup
        -developer -developerkeys to the shortcut or to Steam’s launch options,
        and some
        need the Max Payne 2 Weapon Patch first.
  - id: fusionfix
    year: Max Payne 3
    title: FusionFix
    meta: ThirteenAG’s fixes and extra settings
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        ThirteenAG’s MaxPayne3.FusionFix adds options and fixes problems in
        Max Payne 3; it needs the Complete Edition, version 1.0.0.255 or later.
        It adds a borderless window, controller icons for Xbox, PlayStation,
        Switch and Steam Deck pads, an intro skip, a hidden skip prompt in
        cutscenes, thicker subtitle outlines, SMAA antialiasing and the gamma
        of the console versions. It also stops the Hoboken Alleys co-op map
        from crashing the game and the game from dropping into the pause menu
        at random.
      - >-
        Unpack the archive into the game’s root folder; the ASI Loader it needs
        comes with it.
  - id: sam-lake-mod
    year: Max Payne 3
    title: Sam Lake’s face, back on Max
    meta: AlexSavvy’s Sam Lake Mod, 2023
    body:
      - >-
        By July 2023, the modder AlexSavvy had released the Sam Lake Mod,
        which swaps the third game’s Max for a close recreation of Sam Lake,
        the first game’s face model. It replaces 98 models and 66 textures,
        keeps the facial animation and lip sync, follows Max’s hairstyles
        through the story and brings back the first game’s Hawaiian shirt and
        leather jacket.

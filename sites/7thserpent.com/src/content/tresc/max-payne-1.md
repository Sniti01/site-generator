    title: New York, three years after
    meta: Remedy Entertainment · Windows, July 25, 2001 · Metacritic 89 on PC
    art: mp1-k09
    body:
      - >-
        Three years after addicts high on a designer drug called Valkyr killed
        his wife and newborn daughter, Max, a former NYPD detective now with
        the DEA, is undercover inside the Punchinello mob. Framed for a murder,
        he ends up hunted by the police and the mob at once. Remedy told the
        story in comic-book panels with voice-over and built the gunfights
        around bullet time, one of the first games to use the slow-motion
        effect The Matrix had made famous.
  - id: versions
    year: Versions
    title: PC first, then everything else
    meta: North American release dates
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Rockstar brought the game to PlayStation 2 on December 11, 2001 and to
        Xbox on December 18; a Mac OS X version, published by MacSoft,
        followed on July 16, 2002. On the PS2 the levels were cut into smaller
        pieces to fit the console’s memory, with less detail than on PC or
        Xbox. The Game Boy Advance version, out December 16, 2003, changes the
        most: Möbius Entertainment rebuilt it as an isometric shooter that
        keeps the story, many of the comic panels and some of the voice acting.

    title: Where to play it today
    meta: Re-releases, and how long it takes · as of September 2026
    body:
      - >-
        It returned on Xbox 360 as an Xbox Original in 2009, on PS3 as a PS2
        Classic in 2012 and on PlayStation 4 in 2016. As of September 2026 the
        US PlayStation Store lists that PS4 version, which adds trophies and
        1080p rendering and can be played on PS5 too; the Xbox store lists the
        game for Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, where the whole trilogy became
        backward compatible on November 15, 2021; and Steam carries it on PC.
        HowLongToBeat puts the main story at about 8 hours and everything at
        about 11½.

        the PC game ported by War Drum Studios, with HD graphics as the only
        big change. It came to iOS in April 2012 and to Android on June 14,
        2012. As of September 2026 the App Store’s latest version is 2.0 from
        October 17, 2018, rated 18+, and Google Play shows an update from June
        2, 2026; the Android listing names select USB gamepads among supported
        controllers.

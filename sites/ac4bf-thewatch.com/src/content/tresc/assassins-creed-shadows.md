    year: '2025'
    title: The fourteenth game — Japan, 1579, two playable heroes
    meta: Ubisoft Québec · March 20, 2025 · PC, PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2
    art: japonia
    flip: false
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        Assassin’s Creed Shadows came out on March 20, 2025 on Windows,
        PlayStation 5, Xbox Series and macOS, and on December 2 of the same
        year on Nintendo Switch 2. It is the fourteenth game in the main
        series, the successor to Mirage, made by Ubisoft Québec. The story
        begins in 1579, at the end of the Sengoku period, as Oda Nobunaga
        unifies the country by force.
      - >-
        Naoe is a shinobi from Iga Province, the fictional daughter of
        Fujibayashi Nagato; Yasuke is an African warrior in Nobunaga’s
        service and the series’ first playable hero taken from history. They
        start on opposite sides: Nobunaga’s armies invade Iga, defended by
        the Iga-ikki league, which in the game is the Japanese branch of the
        Assassin Brotherhood. Their common enemy turns out to be the
        Shinbakufu, the local counterpart of the Templars.
  - id: od-red-do-premiery
    year: 2022–2025
    title: From Codename Red to three release dates
    meta: Announced September 2022 · title May 2024 · release March 2025
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        In September 2022, at the Ubisoft Forward showcase, two games were
        announced at once: Codename Red and Codename Hexe. The game
        circulated in leaks as Red until May 2024, when the gameplay, the
        title Shadows and a release date of November 15, 2024 were shown.
        That date was not kept: in September 2024 the release was pushed to
        February 14, 2025, and in January 2025 — a second time — to March
        20. That is why “Assassin’s Creed 2024” still circulates in search
        engines.
      - >-
        There is no version for PlayStation 4 or Xbox One: it is the first
        game built from the ground up for ninth-generation consoles, and
        producer Karl Onnée explained that the older consoles could not
        handle the new version of the Anvil engine with dynamic lighting
        and changing weather.
  - id: naoe-i-yasuke
    year: Naoe
    title: 'Naoe and Yasuke: two styles, not two outfits'
    meta: Shinobi · samurai · switching after the first act
    art: japonia-k05
    flip: false
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        Switching between two characters returns for the first time since
        Syndicate from 2015. After the first act you can change characters
        almost at will, and according to the developers about ninety
        percent of the game can be completed with either one. Naoe is
        small, fast and quiet: she climbs rocks and walls, uses a grappling
        hook, hides in shadow, puts out lanterns with a shuriken, crawls
        along the ground and hides in shallow water, breathing through
        a bamboo tube.
      - >-
        Yasuke is strength: he breaks gates open with a charge, blocks and
        parries, fights with a katana, a naginata and a kanabō, and shoots
        a bow and an arquebus. On the other hand he climbs and sneaks
        poorly — he gets into a castle by ladder or through the main gate.
        In the options you can restore an old series convention: a one-hit
        kill with the hidden blade instead of merely wounding the enemy, as
        in the newer games.
  - id: swiat-i-pory-roku
    year: Sengoku
    title: Central Japan and the four seasons
    meta: Ōmi, Iga, Yamato, Kii · Kyoto, Ōsaka, Lake Biwa
    art: foto-japonia
    flip: true
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        The map covers several provinces of central Japan — Ōmi, Iga, Yamato
        and Kii — with Kyoto, Ōsaka and Lake Biwa; in size the world is
        close to Origins’ Egypt. Azuchi and Ōsaka castles and the Honnō-ji
        and Kiyomizu-dera temples have been recreated; the historical
        figures include Toyotomi Hideyoshi, Tokugawa Ieyasu, Hattori Hanzō
        and Akechi Mitsuhide.
      - >-
        The seasons change about every two hours of play and change the
        rules: in spring and summer you can vanish in tall grass or dive
        from a height into water, while in winter icicles knocked off roofs
        give a climber away. For the first time in the series light and
        darkness are a mechanic — in shadow Naoe is invisible — and
        nightingale floors creak in the castles. The hideout returns in its
        Valhalla form, but you place buildings wherever you like, and scouts
        bring in materials at the end of each season.

        2025: Naoe searches Awaji Island for her missing mother, and traps

        update. The expansion was received more coolly than the base game.
      - >-
        Free quests followed the release: in May 2025 a crossover with Dead
        by Daylight and Balatro, in November a quest with Kassandra from
        Odyssey and — available only until December 22 — a crossover with
        the manga Attack on Titan, with a free mission and paid costumes.
        Hence “Assassin’s Creed titans” in search engines.
  - id: werdykt
    year: Verdict
    title: Who Shadows is for, and who should pick Mirage instead
    meta: About 30 hours for the main story · 75–80 with everything else
    art: japonia-k07
    flip: true
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        Shadows is a game for someone who wants Japan and stealth in the
        shadows. There is more stealth here than in Odyssey and Valhalla,
        and Naoe is a character built for it from the start. Yasuke is for
        those drawn to open combat, but the heart of this game is castles
        and stealth. Reviewers generally received the game well, but
        criticized its bland main story and repetitive quests, and after
        a few dozen hours the sheer size of the map starts to weigh on
        players.
      - >-
        The main story takes about thirty hours, all the activities
        seventy-five to eighty. If you want a shorter, tighter game in the
        classic form, there is Mirage; if you want a big world for the long
        haul, there is Shadows. You can start with it: it requires no
        earlier game, and the modern-day story sits in the Animus Hub, in
        files to read.

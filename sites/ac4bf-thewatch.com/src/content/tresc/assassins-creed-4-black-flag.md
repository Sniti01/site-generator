verdict:
  label: Verdict
  body:
    - >-
      The best installment for someone who came for the world, not for the
      stealth. Sailing carries the whole game here and keeps the pace better
      than the missions on land.
    - >-
      If you are looking for classic Assassin’s Creed with a city and
      rooftops, start with II or Revelations — here the cities are stops
      between voyages.

      - >-
        The Jackdaw is the second protagonist here, and half of the playtime
        belongs to it: chases, broadside-to-broadside fire, boarding by rope.
        The cities — Havana, Kingston, Nassau — remain, but have shrunk to
        stops between voyages. If you came for sneaking across rooftops, you
        get less of it than in Revelations.
      - >-
        Story-wise it is a prequel to III: Edward is Connor’s grandfather,
        and the modern-day story moves into the offices of Abstergo. Rogue
        tells the same era from the other side — which is why the two games
        are read as a pair, not separately.
  - id: edward-kenway
    year: '1715'
    title: Edward Kenway — a privateer in someone else’s robes
    meta: Swansea · Havana · the Observatory · the Sage
    art: karaiby-k06
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Edward Kenway comes from Swansea in Wales and arrives in the
        Caribbean as a privateer who has run out of war, not out of appetite
        for profit. In June 1715, after the disaster off Cape Bonavista, he
        kills an Assassin he does not know, takes his robes and a letter,
        and shows up in Havana as Duncan Walpole. The sender of the letter,
        Laureano Torres, governor of Cuba, welcomes the newcomer into the
        Templars and shows him the Sage — a man whose blood opens the
        Observatory, a device for watching people from afar. From then on
        Edward hunts for the Observatory because he wants to sell it, not
        because he believes in either side.
      - >-
        Then come Nassau and the Pirate Republic with Blackbeard, Benjamin
        Hornigold and Charles Vane, the epidemic in Nassau in 1718, the
        collapse of the republic and Blackbeard’s death. The Sage turns out
        to be Bartholomew Roberts; he leads Edward to the Observatory and
        betrays him there. In 1720 Kenway gets out of prison with Anne
        Bonny, but without Mary Read. Only then does he join the Brotherhood,
        and in 1721 he is given three targets: Woodes Rogers, Torres and
        Roberts. The story closes with a scene at the opera in England, with
        his daughter Jennifer and his son Haytham — where Assassin’s Creed
        III begins.
  - id: kawka
    year: Jackdaw
    title: The Jackdaw — a brig you have to capture and upgrade
    meta: Cannon fire · boarding · your own fleet · shanties
    art: karaiby-k01
    flip: false
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        Edward steals the Jackdaw from the Spanish fleet while escaping from
        Havana, together with Adéwalé, and from that moment the ship is the
        axis of the game. At first the brig can only handle the weakest
        vessels; broadside cannons, mortars, swivel guns, the ram and a
        reinforced hull are bought with reales and goods, and goods are
        taken from captured ships.
      - >-
        The end of a battle is a choice: sink the ship and take less, or
        board it and complete tasks on deck — cut down a set number of the
        crew, blow up the powder store. A captured ship can be added to your
        own fleet, which you send on voyages for resources in a separate
        minigame, used to lower your notoriety so the bounty hunters leave
        you alone, or used to patch up the Jackdaw. On top of that there are
        storms, a harpoon for whales and sharks, and the shanties the crew
        sings at sea — reviewers named them among the strongest elements of
        the game.
  - id: trzy-miasta
    year: Caribbean
    title: Havana, Nassau, Kingston and more than fifty islands
    meta: The Spanish · the British · the pirates
    art: foto-karaiby
    flip: true
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        The map is the West Indies from southern Florida to Tulum in Mexico:
        three large cities and more than fifty smaller locations —
        plantations, smugglers’ dens, forts, Mayan ruins, wrecks to dive to.
        Havana is held by the Spanish, Kingston by the British, Nassau by
        the pirates. On Great Inagua Edward takes over the hideout of a
        Templar he has killed; the Assassins have their base in Tulum.
      - >-
        On land the game is close to III: parkour and combat unchanged, the
        arsenal shrunk to cutlasses, rapiers and flintlock pistols. Stealth
        has changed more: there is a blowpipe and more missions of tailing
        and eavesdropping — the ones reviewers criticized most often.
        Viewpoints, chests and Templar keys remain: the full set of keys from
        the Templar Hunts unlocks the Templar Armor in the mansion on Great
        Inagua.
  - id: abstergo
    year: Abstergo
    title: The modern-day story without Desmond, and multiplayer
    meta: Abstergo Entertainment · first person · Game Lab
    art: karaiby-k03
    flip: false
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        After III, in which Desmond Miles dies, Abstergo extracts his DNA,
        and from then on ancestral memories are read from the cloud. The
        player is a nameless employee of Abstergo Entertainment, which is
        gathering material for an interactive film about pirates; officially
        it is about entertainment, in practice the Templars are looking for
        the Observatory. These sections are played in first person: an
        office, terminals you break into through minigames, overheard
        conversations.
      - >-
        Reviewers generally received this story well, though not without
        reservations: for some it was a break from pirate life, if an
        unnecessary one; for others a mostly optional extra they hurried
        through to get back to the Caribbean. Standing apart was the
        multiplayer mode, with the target-and-hunter rule known from earlier
        games and the Game Lab editor. The remake has neither.

        Freedom Cry came out in December 2013 as DLC and in February 2014
        as a standalone game. Its hero is Adéwalé, the Jackdaw’s
        quartermaster, more than a decade later and by now an Assassin; the
        action takes place around Saint-Domingue, a machete and a
        blunderbuss replace the cutlass and pistol, and the new mechanic is
        freeing slaves. It is nine missions in the same form as Black Flag.
        Alongside it came three more missions for Aveline de Grandpré from
        Liberation, at first only on PlayStation.
      - >-
        Rogue, released on November 11, 2014, rounds off the story of the
        Kenways: Haytham and Adéwalé return in it, and one of its sections
        ties in with the prologue of Unity. Its hero is Shay Cormac, an
        Assassin who goes over to the Templars.

    body:
      - >-
        Release on PlayStation 3, Xbox 360 and Wii U: October 29, 2013 in
        North America, November 1 in Europe; PlayStation 4 — November 15,
        PC — November 19, Xbox One — November 22. On Switch the game came
        out on December 6, 2019 in The Rebel Collection bundle, together
        with Rogue. By May 2014 more than 11 million copies had been sold.
        On Steam the game is sold today as the Gold Edition: the base game,
        Freedom Cry, the Illustrious Pirates Pack and cosmetic packs.
      - >-
        Ubisoft confirmed the remake, titled Assassin’s Creed Black Flag
        Resynced, on March 4, 2026, showed it on April 23 and released it on
        July 9, 2026 on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. Combat has been
        rebuilt around parrying, stealth and parkour improved; added are
        storylines for Blackbeard and Stede Bonnet, three officers in the
        main story and a photo mode. There is no multiplayer and there are
        no Abstergo sequences.
  - id: dla-kogo
    year: Start
    title: Who it’s for and where to start
    meta: A standalone entry point · 20 hours of story · before III or after it
    art: karaiby-k05
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Black Flag is one of three good entry points into the series,
        alongside II and Origins: the historical story wraps up within its
        own era, and the modern-day one starts a new form here, so you don’t
        need to know anything that came before. The order of the eras is the
        reverse of the release order — Black Flag is 1715, Rogue 1752, III
        1754 — and the first time through it is better to stick to the
        release order.
      - >-
        The main story takes about twenty hours; all the collectibles and
        activities, sixty to eighty. If the Caribbean pulls you in, next in
        line are Freedom Cry, then Rogue. Between the original and Resynced:
        the original has multiplayer, the Abstergo story and a lower price;
        the remake has new combat, new storylines and new visuals, without
        those two things.

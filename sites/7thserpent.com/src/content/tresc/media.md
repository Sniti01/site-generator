      - >-
        The Max Payne 3 cover went public on March 7, 2012, two months before
        the game reached shelves. It reworks the art from the announcement the
        previous fall: a battle-scarred Max in front of São Paulo’s skyline and
        its helicopters, with the city’s criminals and a Brazilian woman
        around him.
      - >-
        The series logo sits on Wikimedia Commons, taken from Remedy’s page for
        the remake and marked as a possible trademark. A font blog names
        Adobe’s Machine Medium as the typeface of the logo.
  - id: graphic-novel
    year: Graphic novel
    title: A story told in comic panels
    meta: The first two games, and the Max Payne 3 comics
    art: mp2-k01
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Both Remedy games tell most of their story in graphic-novel panels with
        voice-over. The developers found panels cheaper and more effective than
        animated cutscenes, easier to reorder when the plot changed, and open
        to the player’s own reading. Sam Lake modeled for
        Max in the first game; actor Timothy Gibbs took over in Max Payne 2.
      - >-
        Around the launch of Max Payne 3, a three-part comic series made with
        Marvel Custom Solutions went back over the first two games and Max’s
        past. It was collected as Max Payne 3: The Complete Series in October
        2013.
  - id: screenshots-and-art
    year: Art
    title: Screenshots, concept art and prints
    meta: Where the images come from
    art: mp1-k13
    flip: true
    body:
      - >-
        The frames on this site are screenshots and key art from the games’
        Steam pages, toned to the site’s colors. Max Payne 2, with Saku
        Lehtinen credited as its artist, took the 2004 Golden Satellite Award
        for Outstanding Art Direction.
      - >-
        For Max Payne 3 the credits list artists Paul MacPherson, Joshua Bass
        and Rob Nelson, and Anthrox Studio names itself for the concept art
        and the marketing art direction, with large photo shoots and actors.
        The game’s Special Edition came with a set of still-life art prints.
  - id: soundtrack
    year: Soundtrack
    title: From Late Goodbye to HEALTH
    meta: The music of all three games
    art: mp3-k15
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Kärtsy Hatakka and Kimmo Kajasto scored the first two games. Max Payne
        2 adds cello by Perttu Kivilaakso of Apocalyptica and ends on Late
        Goodbye by Poets of the Fall, its lyrics based on a poem by Sam Lake.
      - >-
        Max Payne 3 belongs to the Los Angeles noise-rock band HEALTH. The
        album came out on May 23, 2012, the launch trailer carried MAX: THEME,
        and at the 2012 Spike Video Game Awards the score was nominated for
        Best Score and TEARS for Best Song in a Game.

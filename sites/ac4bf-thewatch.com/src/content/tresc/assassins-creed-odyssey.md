verdict:
  label: Verdict
  body:
    - >-
      The best choice for someone who came for the world and a story of
      their own rather than for stealth. Greece is colorful, the sea returns
      in good form, and the choice of hero and dialogue lines gives the
      family story a weight that Origins lacked.
    - >-
      The weaknesses are well known: pacing held in check by levels, an
      overloaded map and paid experience boosters. If you want a classic
      Assassin with a hidden blade, go in through Assassin’s Creed II or
      Mirage; if you want to stay in Greece for dozens of hours, get Gold
      and start on Kephallonia.

      - >-
        Assassin’s Creed Odyssey came out on PC, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One
        on October 5, 2018. It was made by Ubisoft Québec — the studio behind
        Syndicate and, later, Shadows. The action takes place in Greece in
        431–422 BCE, during the war between Sparta and Athens; it is the
        earliest era in the series, four centuries before the Egypt of
        Origins.
      - >-
        Odyssey continues the turn that Origins began a year earlier —
        levels, skill trees, loot — and adds what the series never had: a
        choice of hero and of dialogue lines, and several endings. On
        Metacritic it scores 86 on PC.

    title: Alexios or Kassandra — an outcast with the blood of Leonidas
    meta: Kephallonia · Nikolaos · Cult of Kosmos · Deimos · Myrrine
    art: assassins-creed-odyssey-k05
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        At the start you choose a grandson or granddaughter of King
        Leonidas. The child of this bloodline is thrown off Mount Taygetos,
        survives and grows up on Kephallonia as a misthios — a mercenary
        with no master. The first big contract is to kill the “Wolf of
        Sparta”, that is Nikolaos — the father who threw the child off; this
        mission starts a journey to find the family: the mother, Myrrine,
        and the sibling whom the cult raised as its champion, Deimos.
      - >-
        The enemy is the Cult of Kosmos — an organization that knows whose
        blood the hero carries and has influence all over Greece. Choices
        don’t change the history of Greece — the plague in Athens always
        happens — but they decide who in the family survives. The developers
        spoke of a Greek tragedy and recorded more than thirty hours of
        cutscenes with dialogue choices.

    title: Greece, the Spear of Leonidas, mercenaries and the ship Adrestia
    meta: Athens · Sparta · Cult of Kosmos · 150-on-150 battles · Ikaros
    art: assassins-creed-odyssey-k04
    flip: false

    body:
      - >-
        The map covers almost all of Greece — Attica with Athens, Lakonia
        with Sparta, Delphi, Olympia, Crete and the islands, between which
        you sail on the ship Adrestia, acquired while still on Kephallonia,
        where the game starts. Every region has a recommended level. Along
        the way you meet Socrates, Herodotus and Pericles, while Medusa, the
        Cyclops and the Minotaur are bosses explained as Isu experiments.
      - >-
        The hidden blade is replaced by the head of the Spear of Leonidas;
        you upgrade it with fragments taken from members of the
        Cult of Kosmos — forty-two cultists with the Ghost of Kosmos at
        their head. There are three skill trees: Hunter, Warrior and
        Assassin; there is no shield. Break the law and mercenaries come
        after you. A weakened region opens a conquest battle on the side of
        Athens or Sparta, with 150 warriors on each side; the side you pick
        does not change the story.
  - id: origins-i-layla
    year: 2017–2018
    title: How Odyssey differs from Origins — and how Layla links them
    meta: Bayek and Egypt · pacing and levels · Layla Hassan · Staff of Hermes
    art: assassins-creed-origins
    flip: true
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        Origins rebuilt the series: the eagle, levels, loot, open combat.
        Odyssey takes that skeleton and adds a choice of hero, dialogue,
        romances and conquests. Bayek founded the order and its creed;
        the hero of Odyssey lives before them and works for whoever pays.
        The price of that freedom is pacing: the main story keeps demanding
        a level you don’t have, and Ubisoft sold paid packs that speed up
        experience — reviewers criticized both.
      - >-
        The modern-day story links the two games: Layla Hassan, a former
        Abstergo researcher from Origins, finds the Spear of Leonidas,
        extracts the DNA of the king’s grandchildren from it and uses a
        portable Animus to search for the Staff of Hermes and Atlantis.
        Valhalla wraps up her story, and on December 14, 2021, both games
        received Crossover Stories for free, with a meeting between Eivor
        and Kassandra.

        the First Blade (December 2018 – March 2019) and The Fate of

        Assassin’s Creed III Remastered with Liberation. Free additions were
        Lost Tales of Greece, New Game Plus and the Discovery Tour from
        2019.
      - >-
        Editions on Steam: Standard, Deluxe, Gold with the season pass, and
        Ultimate with both; the game requires a Ubisoft Connect account and
        46 GB of space, PEGI 18. There is no PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series
        release — the game runs through backward compatibility, and a patch
        from August 2021 brought 60 frames per second. On September 15,
        2022, Odyssey joined Game Pass. The Switch version is Japan-only and
        runs in the cloud.

    body:

        Odyssey is for the player who wants a big open-world RPG: weeks in
        Greece, dialogue choices, sailing and hunting down the cult. Player
        statistics put the average at thirty-eight hours of play and around
        one hundred and forty to complete it.
      - >-
        You can start the series with Odyssey: its era is the earliest, the
        Greek story is self-contained, and the order does not exist yet.
        Layla’s storyline reads better after Origins, though — the guide on
        where to start offers two routes: release order or the order of
        eras, in which Odyssey comes first.

  - id: the-voice
    year: The voice
    title: James McCaffrey
    meta: Max in all three games, 2001 to 2012
    body:
      - >-
        James McCaffrey voiced Max in all three games, released in 2001, 2003
        and 2012. For Max Payne 3 he also did the motion capture and gave Max
        his face, though Rockstar had first announced a recast with an older
        actor. McCaffrey compared the third game’s Max to Charles Bronson’s Paul
        Kersey in Death Wish, and the motion-capture work to acting in Avatar.
      - >-
        In Remedy’s Alan Wake series he voiced both Thomas Zane and Alex Casey,
        and in Control he was Director Trench. An Albany, New York, native born
        in 1958, McCaffrey died of multiple myeloma on December 17, 2023, aged
        65, at home in Larchmont.
  - id: the-first-face
    year: The first face
    title: Sam Lake
    meta: Max Payne, 2001 — writer and face model
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Sam Lake, born Sami Järvi, wrote the first two games and is Remedy’s
        creative director; the character is his creation. The first game had
        no money for actors, so Lake and other Remedy staff played the roles,
        and photos of Lake’s face became Max’s. Lake’s mother played Nicole
        Horne and his father Alfred Woden, and Lake dressed up as Max for the
        comic-panel cutscenes.
      - >-
        The famous grimace has a simple origin, Lake explained in 2023: Max’s
        face swaps to a squint when he fires, and the game’s screenshots all
        caught him firing, so that squint is the face everyone saw.
        In Alan Wake 2, Lake lends his face and motion capture to Alex Casey.
  - id: the-second-face
    year: The second face
    title: Timothy Gibbs
    meta: Max Payne 2, 2003
    body:
      - >-
        For the sequel, a bigger budget let Remedy hire professional actors for
        the comic panels, and Timothy Gibbs became Max’s face while Lake stuck
        to writing. The new face does more, too: in Max Payne 2, Max often
        smirks and moves his eyebrows. Lake still turns up in the sequel,
        playing characters in its TV shows and on its billboards.
  - id: the-third-face
    year: The third face
    title: Why Max went bald
    meta: Max Payne 3, 2012
    art: mp3-k10
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Rockstar made the third game’s Max older, bald and bearded, “more
        world-weary and cynical than ever,” and fans and critics reacted badly.
        Rockstar’s answer was a look that changes over the game, with the
        classic Max in the New Jersey flashbacks. In the story, Max shaves his
        head himself: after the attack that kills his employer, Rodrigo Branco,
        he blames himself and quits drinking.
      - >-
        On PC, a 2023 fan mod by AlexSavvy puts Sam Lake’s face on the third
        game’s Max — more on the mods page.
  - id: max-payne-3-cast

  - id: the-creators
    year: The creators
    title: Who made Max Payne, and who owns it
    meta: From a Finnish studio to Rockstar
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Remedy Entertainment, a Finnish studio in Espoo started in 1995 by
        people from the demoscene group Future Crew, made the first two games;
        3D Realms signed the first one and backed it. On December 5, 2001, Take-Two
        bought the series from Remedy and 3D Realms for $10 million and 970,000
        shares. Its label Rockstar Games published the sequel and built Max
        Payne 3 in its own studios, with Remedy consulting near the end.
      - >-
        As of September 2026, the rights are still Rockstar’s, and Remedy is
        remaking the first two games as a single title on its Northlight
        engine, with Rockstar funding and publishing it.
  - id: remake-cast
    year: The remake
    title: Who will play Max next?
    meta: As of September 2026, Remedy leaves any news to Rockstar
    body:
      - >-
        As of September 2026, Remedy is keeping quiet about the Max Payne 1 & 2
        Remake: in August it confirmed that any announcement is up to Rockstar.
        In October 2023, two months before McCaffrey died, TechRadar reported
        Lake telling Eurogamer that it was too soon to know whether his own
        face would return.

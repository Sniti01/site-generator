verdict:
  label: Verdict
  body:
    - >-
      Origins is for those who want a big world and have the time for it.
      Egypt is the game’s strongest point: the cities differ from one
      another, and Bayek is a grown, experienced hero whose story with Aya
      holds you to the end. Critics praised the world, the combat and the
      characters, and faulted the uneven pacing and repetitive side quests.
    - >-
      If you love the old Assassin’s Creed games for stealth and rooftops,
      you get less here: the hidden blade does not kill everyone, and the
      road to a target runs through levels. Such players are better off
      coming in through II or Mirage.

      - >-
        Assassin’s Creed Origins came out on October 27, 2017, on PC,
        PlayStation 4 and Xbox One. It is the tenth main-line game and the
        successor to Syndicate. Ubisoft Montreal worked on it for four
        years: after Syndicate’s weaker sales, the publisher skipped its
        yearly release for the first time since II.
      - >-
        The game is set in 49–44 BCE, at the end of the Ptolemaic dynasty:
        Ptolemy XIII holds the throne, the sidelined Cleopatra gathers
        allies, and Caesar’s legions stand at the gates. The hero is Bayek
        of Siwa, a Medjay, a guardian from an old Egyptian tradition.

      - >-
        In Siwa, masked men drag Bayek and his son Khemu into a vault
        beneath the Temple of Amun and order the father to open it with a
        Piece of Eden; the boy dies in the struggle. A year later Bayek is
        hunting the kidnappers, and Aya, his wife, leads him to Cleopatra:
        the masks are worn by members of the Order of the Ancients, and
        Ptolemy’s throne is their front.
      - >-
        Once Cleopatra takes the throne, she allies herself with the Order.
        Then Bayek and Aya found the Hidden Ones — the organization the
        series treats as the beginning of the Brotherhood; Aya travels to
        Rome and takes the name Amunet. In the present day, Abstergo
        researcher Layla Hassan reads their memories on a modified Animus
        and stays on for the next two games.
  - id: egipt-i-walka
    year: Egypt
    title: One map without sectors, Senu and hitbox combat
    meta: Alexandria · Memphis · Giza · hunter, warrior, seer
    art: assassins-creed-origins-k03
    flip: false
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        The map is the size of Black Flag’s Caribbean, with land instead of
        water. Alexandria, Memphis, the pyramids of Giza and Cyrene form a
        single area without loading sectors; you ride a horse or a camel,
        sail a felucca and dive. Eagle Vision was replaced by Senu, an eagle
        that marks enemies from above, and the minimap by a compass.
      - >-
        Paired animations with scripted counters gave way to hitboxes: a
        weapon hits or misses, and you block blows with a shield. Eight
        types of melee weapons and four types of bows come in rarity tiers,
        and levels unlock hunter, warrior and seer abilities. Quests have a
        recommended level, and the hidden blade does not kill stronger
        enemies.

  - id: remedy
    year: Remedy
    title: Remedy’s own games
    meta: The studio that made the first two Max Payne games
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Remedy Entertainment made Max Payne and Max Payne 2, and its other
        games include Alan Wake, Alan Wake 2, Quantum Break and Control. The
        ties to Max run through people. James McCaffrey, the voice of Max in
        all three games, played Alex Casey in both Alan Wake games and
        Zachariah Trench in Control. In Alan Wake 2, Sam Lake, whose face Max
        wore in the first game, plays Alex Casey on screen.
  - id: story-first
    year: Story first
    title: If it was Max himself that got to you
    meta: Answers to a Steam thread asking for similar games, story-wise
    body:
      - >-
        The thread opened in October 2025 with a grown man who recognized
        himself in Max and asked for games with leads like him. One reply alone
        listed Final Fantasy VII, Persona 5 Royal, GTA IV, Fallout: New Vegas,
        Silent Hill 1 and 2, Alan Wake 1 and 2, NieR: Automata and three Metal
        Gear Solid games (3, 4 and Phantom Pain). Others added Yakuza 0 with
        the rest of the Kiryu games, and BioShock came up in passing.
      - >-
        Days Gone came with a reason that sounds like Max: a hero who searches
        for his wife while grieving her. Another reply grouped the first The
        Last of Us, The Suffering and The Darkness as gritty personal dramas,
        with the warning that all three bring zombies or the paranormal and
        are nothing really like Max Payne. One answer simply said that Max
        Payne 3 has no equal.
  - id: rawg-shooters
    year: Gameplay
    title: The shooters on RAWG’s list
    meta: Matched on gameplay or visual style, with years and studios
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        RAWG builds its list of games like Max Payne 3 from gameplay or visual
        style. The shooters on its first page: Hitman: Absolution (2012, IO
        Interactive), Metal Gear Solid V: Ground Zeroes (2014, Kojima
        Productions), Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine (2011, Relic
        Entertainment), Sniper Elite 4 (2017, Rebellion) and DOOM Eternal
        (2020, id Software among others). Tom Clancy’s Splinter Cell Chaos
        Theory (2005) and Resident Evil 3 (2020) join them as action-adventures.
  - id: the-next-max-payne
    year: What’s next
    title: "The next Max Payne is a remake"
    meta: Max Payne 1 & 2 Remake, as of September 2026
    body:
      - >-
        Remedy is remaking the first two games as a single game, Max Payne 1 &
        2 Remake, on its Northlight engine; Rockstar Games funds and publishes
        it for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. As of September 2026 it
        is still in development and has no release date.
      - >-
        The author of that Steam thread, who had played the series from the
        first game on, left a note worth keeping: some of the dialogue in Max
        Payne 3 is hard to follow if you skipped the earlier games.

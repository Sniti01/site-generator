    body:
      - >-
        The fourteen main-line games have covered eras from the Greece of
        Odyssey, 431–422 BCE, to the London of 1868 in Syndicate. There the
        list breaks off: the 20th century appears in it only on the margins,
        and World War II as a standalone game — not at all.
      - >-
        Players have been asking for this era for years — a thread on the
        Steam forums discusses why World War II will never fit the series —
        and gaming sites return to the topic whenever a clip from Unity or
        Syndicate makes the rounds online.
  - id: co-jest-w-serii
    year: '1916'
    title: What exists — London 1916 in Syndicate and occupied Paris in Unity
    meta: Lydia Frye · Churchill · time anomalies · the Eiffel Tower · Russia 1918
    art: londyn
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Only World War I got missions of its own. In Syndicate, an anomaly
        in the Animus takes the Initiate to London in 1916: you play as
        Lydia Frye, Jacob’s granddaughter, who breaks up a German spy ring
        on Churchill’s orders and finds Templars inside it. It is a side
        memory, unlocked from sequence six.
      - >-
        World War II is only an episode. In Unity, time anomalies throw Arno
        into other eras, and one of them is occupied Paris: a climb up the
        Eiffel Tower and a machine gun against German planes. Ubisoft showed
        this segment just before the launch in 2014. The only full game set
        in the 20th century is Chronicles: Russia — Russia in 1918, a 2.5D
        stealth game.
  - id: dlaczego-trudno
    year: Weapons
    title: What makes it hard for the series to enter this era
    meta: Automatic weapons · cars · the historical record · memory
    art: foto-paryz-1940
    flip: false
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        The series runs on a city with rooftops, a crowd you can disappear
        into and a hidden blade at close range. Players opposed to the idea
        point on forums to submachine guns, which make a quiet drop from a
        rooftop pointless, and to cars, which leave no reason to climb onto
        rooftops at all. A third argument from the same place: World War II
        is the best-documented conflict in history, and the series lives on
        a fictional conspiracy inside real events — the denser the record,
        the less room for an Assassin.
      - >-
        There is also memory — opponents point out that the series puts its
        hero in the middle of real events, and those of 1939–1945 are no
        backdrop for playing at assassins. Ubisoft drew accusations from the
        French Left Party over its portrayal of the Revolution in Unity —
        and that is an era centuries back. Others reply that World War I,
        with its trenches and bolt-action rifles, would fit better;
        Syndicate showed that it works on a small scale.
  - id: co-dalej
    year: '2027'
    title: What comes next, and what circulates as “World War II”
    meta: Resynced · Hexe · Invictus · TeaserPlay concept · the year 1492
    art: karaiby-k04
    flip: true
    band: false
    body:
      - >-
        The announcements point elsewhere: in July 2026 Black Flag Resynced
        came out, a remake of the fourth game; Hexe, set in Germany in the
        16th or 17th century, is planned for 2027; Invictus is to focus on
        online play.
      - >-
        What circulates online as “Assassin’s Creed: World War II” is
        fan-made: a concept trailer from the TeaserPlay channel with
        occupied Paris in Unreal Engine 5, described by its creators as a
        vision, not a game, or the drawing “Uprising 1944” on DeviantArt.
        And “1492” is not a missing era but the year of Columbus’s voyage:
        the Spain of Discovery on the DS and the 2016 film, whose historical
        part is set in Andalusia.

verdict:
  label: Verdict
  body:
    - >-
      There is no full-fledged game about World War II, and none has been
      announced. Two traces remain: the rift in Unity with occupied Paris
      and — from World War I — Lydia Frye’s missions in the London of 1916
      in Syndicate.
    - >-
      If the 20th century matters to you, Syndicate gives you the most: a
      good game about London with a World War I episode inside. If you are
      looking for occupied Paris, Unity gives you one mission, not a
      campaign. If you want the latest game, there is Shadows and Japan in
      1579.

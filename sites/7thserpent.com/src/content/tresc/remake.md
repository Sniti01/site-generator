    title: Two games, one release
    meta: Max Payne 1 & 2 Remake, as Remedy names it · as of September 2026
    body:
      - >-
        As of September 2026, Max Payne 1 & 2 Remake is one title that covers
        both Max Payne (2001) and Max Payne 2: The Fall of Max Payne (2003).
        Remedy Entertainment, the Finnish studio that made both originals,
        is building it under a development agreement with Rockstar Games, and
        it is not a touch-up: Remedy is remaking both games from scratch on
        Northlight, its in-house engine.
      - >-
        Rockstar owns the series, funds the project and will publish it. As of
        September 2026 Remedy’s page for the game names three platforms:
        PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. Switch, PS4 and Xbox One are not
        on that list.
      - >-
        As of September 2026 the project is also income for Remedy: its report
        for the first quarter of 2026 named the remake and Control Resonant,
        alongside game sales, as its main sources of revenue.

    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        As of September 2026 there is no release date. Rockstar Games handles
        the publishing and every announcement about the project, so Remedy
        answers questions from its investors with a status line and nothing
        more. In the Q&A after its half-year report in August 2026, the
        studio’s investor relations manager said as much: publishing is
        Rockstar’s side.
      - >-
        So, as of September 2026, any date you read elsewhere is somebody’s
        estimate, not an announcement from either company.

verdict:
  label: Verdict
  body:
    - >-
      For a player who wants the biggest game in the series with the
      settlement at its center: according to HowLongToBeat, the main story
      plus side content takes about 99 hours, and 100 percent completion
      149.
    - >-
      It is not worth starting the series with it: Rock Paper Shotgun called
      it a weak entry point because it is too complex. Mirage is its
      prequel — Baghdad in 861 — and is several times shorter; Layla’s
      story in order is Origins, Odyssey, Valhalla. The guide gives both
      orders: by release and by era.

        Assassin’s Creed Valhalla came out on November 10, 2020 on PC,
        PlayStation 4, Xbox One and Series X/S, and on November 12 on
        PlayStation 5. It was made by Ubisoft Montréal, the studio behind
        Origins. Metacritic scores of 80–84; its first week was the best in
        the history of the series.
      - >-
        In 873 Eivor Wolf-Kissed and adoptive brother Sigurd lead the Raven
        Clan out of Norway to England, where they clash with Ælfred of
        Wessex and are drawn into the conflict between the Hidden Ones and
        the Order of the Ancients. Eivor’s gender is chosen at the start or
        left to the Animus — the canonical option. The modern-day story
        concludes Layla Hassan’s arc.
  - id: anglia-i-osada
    year: '873'
    title: Norway, England and Ravensthorpe — region order and the settlement
    meta: Ravensthorpe · alliance map · Gunnar the blacksmith · feast
    art: assassins-creed-valhalla-k02
    flip: true
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Norway is the prologue; in England the clan founds Ravensthorpe and
        picks regions from the alliance map kept there by Randvi. Instead of
        levels there is character power, and each region has a recommended
        power: Ledecestrescire and Grantebridgescire come first, Jorvik and
        Wincestre in the second half. Asgard and Jotunheim are visions from
        the seer Valka.
      - >-
        The settlement returns for the first time since Black Flag: quests
        start and end there, and raids on monasteries supply the materials.
        Eurogamer advises starting with the forge — Gunnar the blacksmith
        raises item quality and unlocks runes — while others recommend the
        Hidden Ones bureau. A feast in the longhouse grants a bonus for
        three hours; the longhouse is also where the dispute between Holger
        and Gudrun is judged (Blame and Sail).
  - id: walka-i-aktywnosci
    year: Orlog
    title: Combat, stealth, orlog and Zealots — what to watch out for at the start
    meta: A weapon in each hand · Odin Sight · Sýnin · no multiplayer
    art: assassins-creed-valhalla-k00
    flip: false

    body:
      - >-
        There are no levels — there is power and a skill tree; there are
        almost no naval battles — the longship can be summoned at any stretch
        of water and mostly serves as transport; there is no multiplayer —
        your Jomsviking appears in other players’ games, controlled by the
        AI. Your hands hold more, though: a weapon in each, even two
        shields, the hidden blade returns, and enemies come in 25
        archetypes. Scouting is done by the raven Sýnin, and Eagle Vision is
        called Odin Sight here.
      - >-
        Orlog is a dice game for two: you knock out your opponent’s health
        stones, a win earns you a new god favor, and there are 19 players to
        face. Zealots, the Order’s mercenaries, roam the roads: each one
        defeated gives a clue to a member of the Order, and you can kill
        them in any order. Animus Anomalies are ten light puzzles; the boss
        of Asgard is the Builder.

        Assassin’s Creed Valhalla on PS5 and Xbox Series is a free upgrade

        2024.
      - >-
        On PC it has been on Ubisoft Connect (Uplay) and Epic since launch
        and on Steam since December 6, 2022; it is not on GOG. Requirements:
        64-bit Windows 10 (not 8.1), a DirectX 12 graphics card (not DX11),
        a GTX 960, 160 GB. Editions: Standard and Gold with the season pass
        at launch; today Deluxe, Ragnarök with Dawn, and Complete. Valhalla
        is free only during free trial weekends.

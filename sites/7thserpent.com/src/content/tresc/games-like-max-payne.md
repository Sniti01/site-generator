      - >-
        Red Dead Redemption 2 is the closest relative in the family sense:
        Rockstar Games developed and published it, and it came out in 2018 on
        PC, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One. Two players in the thread named it: one
        for its cast of compromised, badly flawed people, one because Arthur
        stands as tall as Max.
      - >-
        Spec Ops: The Line (2012) is an action shooter by Yager. Sleeping Dogs,
        in its 2014 Definitive Edition, is an action-adventure from United
        Front Games, Square Enix London Studios and Feral Interactive. Both
        turn up on RAWG’s list and in the same Steam thread.
  - id: remedy
    year: Remedy
    title: Remedy’s own games
    meta: The studio that made the first two Max Payne games
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        Remedy Entertainment made Max Payne and Max Payne 2, and its other
        games include Alan Wake, Alan Wake 2, Quantum Break and Control. The
        ties to Max run through people. James McCaffrey, the voice of Max in
        all three games, played Alex Casey in both Alan Wake games and
        Zachariah Trench in Control. In Alan Wake 2, Sam Lake, whose face Max
        wore in the first game, plays Alex Casey on screen.
  - id: story-first
    year: Story first
    title: If it was Max himself that got to you
    meta: Answers to a Steam thread asking for similar games, story-wise
    body:
      - >-
        The thread opened in October 2025 with a player for whom Max’s struggle
        to get past his trauma hit home. The answers ran long: Metal
        Gear Solid 3, 4 and Phantom Pain, NieR: Automata, Alan Wake 1 and 2,
        Silent Hill 1 and 2, Fallout: New Vegas, GTA IV, Persona 5 Royal,
        Final Fantasy VII and the BioShock games, plus Yakuza 0 and the other
        Yakuza games with Kiryu.
      - >-
        Some picks came with reasons that sound like Max: Days Gone, whose hero
        loses track of his injured wife in a pandemic, and the first The Last
        of Us, for its hard personal drama. The Suffering and The Darkness got
        the same praise, with a warning that zombies and the supernatural come
        along. Not everyone played along: one reply said Max Payne 3 has no
        equal.
  - id: rawg-shooters
    year: Gameplay
    title: The shooters on RAWG’s list
    meta: Matched on gameplay or visual style, with years and studios
    band: true
    body:
      - >-
        RAWG builds its list of games like Max Payne 3 from gameplay or visual
        style. The shooters on it: Hitman: Absolution (2012, IO Interactive),
        Metal Gear Solid V: Ground Zeroes (2014, Kojima Productions),
        Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine (2011, Relic Entertainment), Sniper
        Elite 4 (2017, Rebellion) and DOOM Eternal (2020, id Software among
        others). Tom Clancy’s Splinter Cell Chaos Theory (2005) and Resident
        Evil 3 (2020) join them as action-adventures.
  - id: the-next-max-payne
    year: What’s next
    title: The next Max Payne is a remake
    meta: Max Payne 1 & 2 Remake, as of September 2026
    body:
      - >-
        Remedy is remaking the first two games as a single game, Max Payne 1 &
        2 Remake, on its Northlight engine; Rockstar Games funds and publishes
        it for PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. As of September 2026 it
        is still in development and has no release date.
      - >-
        Until then, the author of that Steam thread, who had played the series
        from the first game on, left a note worth keeping: some of the dialogue
        in Max Payne 3 is hard to follow if you skipped the earlier games.
